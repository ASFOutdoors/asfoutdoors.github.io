The Art of Hot Stone Cooking

Cooking meat on a hot stone is one of the oldest and most fascinating methods of outdoor cooking. Heated directly by the campfire, the stone retains heat exceptionally well and allows the steak to develop a beautiful crust while preserving its natural juices.

Without complicated equipment, this primitive cooking technique brings you closer to nature and highlights the simple pleasures of wilderness camping.
