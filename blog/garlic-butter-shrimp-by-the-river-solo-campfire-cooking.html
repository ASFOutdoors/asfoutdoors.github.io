Garlic Butter Shrimp by the River | Solo Campfire Cooking

Published June 2026 • Solo Camping • Campfire Cooking

Cooking by the Riverside

There is something special about preparing a meal beside a flowing river. The sound of moving water, the warmth of a campfire and the beauty of the surrounding wilderness create the perfect setting for outdoor cooking.

On this solo camping adventure, I spent the day relaxing by the river and preparing one of my favorite campfire meals: garlic butter shrimp cooked over an open fire.

Setting Up a Riverside Camp

Finding the right location is an important part of any outdoor adventure. A riverside campsite provides easy access to water, beautiful scenery and a peaceful atmosphere that is difficult to find elsewhere.

After selecting a safe spot above the waterline, I gathered firewood and prepared a comfortable area for cooking. The gentle sound of the river created a relaxing backdrop for the rest of the day.

Building the Campfire

A good campfire is the heart of every outdoor cooking experience. Dry hardwood burns steadily and produces a strong bed of hot coals that is perfect for preparing food.

As the fire grew stronger, the campsite slowly transformed into a cozy outdoor kitchen. The smell of wood smoke filled the air while the river continued to flow quietly nearby.

Preparing Garlic Butter Shrimp

Shrimp is one of the easiest and most flavorful foods to cook over a campfire. Combined with garlic and butter, it creates a simple meal that feels surprisingly luxurious in the wilderness.

Fresh garlic was added to melted butter before the shrimp was placed into the pan. Within minutes, the rich aroma began spreading through the campsite, mixing with the scent of the campfire.

The combination of sizzling butter, garlic and shrimp created an unforgettable outdoor cooking experience. Simple ingredients often produce the best meals when prepared in nature.

The Joy of Solo Campfire Cooking

Cooking outdoors encourages a slower pace and greater appreciation for the process. Without the distractions of everyday life, it becomes easier to focus on the simple pleasures of preparing a meal over a fire.

Every stage of the experience, from gathering firewood to cooking and eating, becomes part of the adventure. This is one reason why solo campfire cooking continues to attract outdoor enthusiasts around the world.

Enjoying the Riverside Atmosphere

While the shrimp cooked, the river provided a constant soundtrack of flowing water. The peaceful surroundings made it easy to relax and enjoy the moment.

Outdoor meals often taste better because they are connected to the experience itself. The effort involved in building a fire and preparing food creates a deeper sense of satisfaction.

A Perfect Wilderness Meal

Once fully cooked, the garlic butter shrimp was ready to enjoy beside the fire. The rich flavor of the butter combined perfectly with the natural sweetness of the shrimp.

Simple campfire recipes like this prove that outdoor cooking does not require complicated ingredients. A few quality ingredients and a reliable campfire are often all that is needed.

Final Thoughts

Garlic butter shrimp cooked beside a river is a perfect example of how rewarding outdoor cooking can be. The combination of fresh food, a warm campfire and beautiful natural surroundings creates an unforgettable wilderness experience.

Whether you enjoy camping, bushcraft or campfire cooking, riverside adventures offer an excellent opportunity to slow down, relax and appreciate the outdoors. Sometimes the best moments in nature come from the simplest meals shared with nothing more than a campfire and the sound of flowing water.
